# TutorMe Final Year Project
## Intro
During my first year I found that sometimes there were some difficulties in understanding a topic which resulted in achieving higher grades of that module much harder.<br>Lecturers were not available all the time due to busy schedule.<br>That made me feeling a bit demotivated and depressed for a short period of time.<br>I wished getting help from a peer who could clearly in an informal environment explain to me the topic so I could move on.<br>Then this year I have suddenly realized that there are so many students expertising in several topics that are always willing to help a buddy.<br>It was clear to me that our university just lacks a platform that could connect supportive students and others who struggle with modules.
